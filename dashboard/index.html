Environmental rules and safety (emission methods)

Score: normalised shares of CO₂, NOx, fuel and PM emissions of the lanes served. The weights set their priorities. Queues and waiting do not count in the score; they only enter the traffic constraints. This reactive score does not guarantee lower emissions.

Change the values, then apply them during the simulation. The values are kept when you change method.

One method is chosen at start. During a run you can switch between the fixed-time and emission methods without resetting the traffic. The map and vehicles are shown in the SUMO window.
